# Standards at a glance

A one-screen comparison of everything tracked in this library. Use it to decide
*which* standard applies to a given concern, then open the standard's page for
detail. Plain-language explanations and audit checklists live on each page.

| [NIST AI RMF](reference_nist_ai_rmf.md "NIST's voluntary AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, NIST AI 100-1, Jan 2023). ## How to read this

- **Binding law** (EU AI Act, DORA) carries hard obligations and penalties. The
  others are **voluntary** — but they are how you *demonstrate* you meet the law,
  and clients and regulators increasingly expect them.
- **Management system vs framework vs guidance:** ISO/IEC 42001 certifies the
  *organisation*; NIST AI RMF gives the risk *vocabulary*; ISO/IEC 23894 gives
  the *how-to*. Most programmes use all three together.
- **One control set, many standards:** in practice you map a single set of
  controls (risk classification, model cards, oversight, monitoring) onto
  whichever standards apply — see each page's *Cross-Framework Mapping*.
