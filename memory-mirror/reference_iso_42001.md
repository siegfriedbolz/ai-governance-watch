# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AI Management System)

!!! info "Provenance"

## Summary

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the world's first certifiable **AI Management System
(AIMS)** standard, published in December 2023. It follows the ISO
management-system structure (Annex SL — like ISO 27001/9001) and specifies
requirements to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an
AIMS, with AI-specific controls in its annexes. Certification is **voluntary**,
typically valid for three years with annual/semi-annual surveillance audits.
The standard itself has not been amended since 2023; the surrounding
**42000-series** is expanding — notably **ISO/IEC 42006** (requirements for
bodies that audit and certify AIMS) at draft (DIS) stage in 2025, plus
**ISO/IEC 23894** (AI risk management guidance) and **ISO/IEC 42005** (AI
system impact assessment).

## Key Sections

- **Clauses 4–10** — the Annex SL management-system spine: context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement.
- **Annex A** — reference set of AI-specific controls (objectives an organisation selects from).
- **Annex B** — implementation guidance for those controls.
- **Annex C** — potential AI-related organisational objectives and risk sources.
- **Annex D** — applying the AIMS across domains/sectors.

## Audit-Relevant Anchors

- **Clause 6.1 + Annex A** — risk assessment/treatment and the Statement of Applicability — the spine of an AIMS audit.
- **Clause 8** — operational planning and control, incl. AI system impact assessment (links to ISO/IEC 42005).
- **Clause 9** — monitoring, internal audit, management review — the recurring evidence base.
- **ISO/IEC 42006** — defines how certification bodies themselves are accredited; relevant to the credibility of any certificate an auditor relies on.
